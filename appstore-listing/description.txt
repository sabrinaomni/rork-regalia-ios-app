You reach for your phone before you reach for God.

You know it. You have promised yourself otherwise. And still, most mornings the feed gets you first, and by the time you look up the day already has its hands on you.

Regalia shuts that door — and puts the key behind a prayer.

THE GUARD — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

Pick the apps, categories and sites that pull hardest at you. With your permission, Regalia uses Apple's Screen Time to lock them across your whole iPhone. Open one and you will not find the feed. You will find Scripture, and a single gold button: Put on the armour.

SEVEN PIECES, EIGHT MINUTES — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

The session walks the Armour of God from Ephesians 6, one piece at a time — the Belt of Truth, the Breastplate of Righteousness, the Sandals of Peace, the Shield of Faith, the Helmet of Salvation, the Sword of the Spirit, and Prayer in the Spirit. Each piece brings its verse, a short word on what it means today, and a line you hold down and declare over yourself.

Finish, and the crown lands. Your apps open for the rest of the day. You did not cave for them — you stood for them. Then, at the hour you choose, the guard closes again and tomorrow starts clean.

A MORNING THAT ASKS HOW YOU ARE — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

Before anything else, Regalia asks how you are feeling, and meets it with Scripture chosen for that — not one verse of the day fired at everyone.

THE ARCHIVE — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

Every verse you are given is kept. Your walk becomes something you can turn back through.

REMINDERS WITHOUT THE GUILT — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

A morning nudge that speaks to you by name. A quieter evening one if the armour is still on the floor. Miss a day and the tone turns to mercy, not shame — no streak-shaming, ever. Three days standing, seven, fourteen, thirty: the mornings after are marked.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

A widget holds today's verse and whether you are standing yet.

WHEN LIFE HAPPENS — REQUIRES SUBSCRIPTION

A few unlock passes each week, for the times you genuinely need in. Spend one and the guard lifts briefly, then closes itself.

Regalia is for anyone who wants their first waking minutes to belong to God instead of an algorithm. Not a streak. A posture.
